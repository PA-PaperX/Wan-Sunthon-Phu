# Design Specification: วันภาษาไทยแห่งชาติ (National Thai Language Day)

## 1. Overview & Theme
- **Concept:** National Thai Language Day celebration & educational quiz.
- **Mood & Tone:** Traditional yet inviting, educational, majestic but accessible for youth.
- **Key Event:** "ตอบคำถามคำผิด - คำถูก" (Thai Word Quiz: Right or Wrong).
- **Institution:** วิทยาลัยเทคนิคนวมินทราชินีมุกดาหาร

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background/Glow:** Cream (`#FDFBF7`) to Soft Gold (`#F4D068`).
- **Primary Text:** Dark Golden Brown (`#8B5A2B` or `#996515`).
- **Accents:** 
  - Light Blue (for contrast, seen on elephant & boy's shirt).
  - Solid Gold (for banners, borders, and decorations).

## 3. Typography
- **Header Font:** Elegant, thick Thai display font (Traditional or Modern Serif Thai).
- **Body Font:** Clean, readable Thai font. 
- **Styling:** Use drop shadows or golden gradients for main headers.

## 4. Key Visual Elements (Assets)
- **Historical Figures/Objects:** 
  - King Ramkhamhaeng the Great (statue holding a scripture).
  - The Ramkhamhaeng Inscription (ศิลาจารึกหลักที่ ๑).
  - Thai Temple/Palace silhouette in the background.
- **Characters:**
  - Blue elephant.
  - Golden/Yellow elephant.
  - Young boy walking between them.
- **Decorations:**
  - Thai traditional borders and corner motifs (ลายกนก/กรอบไทย).
  - Cloud motifs (ลายเมฆ) creating a dreamy, heavenly floor.
  - Sparkles and glowing light effects.

## 5. Layout Structure
- **Top:** Ornate floral motif leading to the main title "วันภาษาไทยแห่งชาติ".
- **Upper-Mid:** Subtitles "แห่งชาติ" and banner with date "๒๙ กรกฎาคม", followed by event name "ตอบคำถามคำผิด - คำถูก".
- **Center/Mid:** Main illustration composition (Statue on left, Inscription on right, Characters at the bottom center).
- **Bottom:** Footer text with the institution name "วิทยาลัยเทคนิคนวมินทราชินีมุกดาหาร", bordered by small Thai motifs.
